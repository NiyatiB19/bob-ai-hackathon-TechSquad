# Presentation Directory (`presentation/`)

This directory will hold the pitch deck and presentation assets for **SupplyGuard AI**.

---

## Planned Presentation Structure

The final presentation slide deck will follow the official hackathon guide and cover:

1. **Problem Statement:**
   - Supply chain disruptions (weather, strikes, geopolitical shocks).
   - Impact on active shipment bottlenecks and idle fleet assets.
   - Risks of late cold-chain temperature excursion detection.

2. **Solution Overview:**
   - SupplyGuard AI platform capability matrix.
   - Intelligent rerouting, fleet redeployment, and sensor telemetry analysis.

3. **Demo & Architecture:**
   - Visual walkthrough of the operational dashboard.
   - System component flow and data pipelines.

4. **IBM Technology Integration:**
   - Implementation of IBM Bob conversational AI decision support.
   - Operational impact of IBM AI technology integration.

5. **Impact & Future Roadmap:**
   - Reduction in cargo spoilage, demurring fees, and delivery delays.
   - Scalability and future feature extensions.
